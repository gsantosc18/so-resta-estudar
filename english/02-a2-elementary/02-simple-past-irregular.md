# Passado Simples — Verbos Irregulares (*Simple Past — Irregular Verbs*)

## Objetivo

Ao final deste tópico, o estudante será capaz de identificar e usar os verbos irregulares mais comuns no passado simples, memorizando suas formas e aplicando-os em contextos reais.

## Pré-requisitos

- [Simple Past — Verbos Regulares](01-simple-past-regular.md)

## Conceitos Fundamentais

### O que são verbos irregulares?

Verbos irregulares **não seguem** o padrão de adicionar *-ed* no passado. Cada verbo tem sua própria forma, que precisa ser memorizada.

> A boa notícia: os ~50 verbos irregulares mais comuns cobrem a maior parte do uso cotidiano.

### Estrutura (igual ao regular)

| Forma | Estrutura | Exemplo |
|-------|----------|---------|
| Afirmativa | Sujeito + **past form** | I **went** to the park. |
| Negativa | Sujeito + **didn't** + base | I **didn't go** to the park. |
| Interrogativa | **Did** + sujeito + base? | **Did** you **go** to the park? |

> **Lembre-se**: Na negativa e interrogativa, o verbo volta à **forma base** (não ao passado):
> - ✅ *Did you **go**?* (forma base)
> - ❌ *Did you **went**?* (passado — ERRADO)

## Regras e Exceções

### Estratégias de Memorização

#### 1. Agrupar por padrão sonoro

| Padrão | Verbos |
|--------|--------|
| -ought/-aught | buy→bought, bring→brought, think→thought, teach→taught, catch→caught |
| -ept | keep→kept, sleep→slept, sweep→swept |
| -elt/-elt | feel→felt, deal→dealt |
| -old/-eld | tell→told, sell→sold, hold→held |
| -eft | leave→left |

#### 2. Verbos que rimam no passado

```
buy → bought    |  bring → brought   | think → thought
(todos com "ought/aught")

keep → kept     |  sleep → slept     | sweep → swept
(todos com "ept")

say → said      |  pay → paid        | lay → laid
(todos com "aid")
```

#### 3. Os 10 mais usados (memorize primeiro!)

1. **be** → was/were
2. **have** → had
3. **do** → did
4. **go** → went
5. **say** → said
6. **get** → got
7. **make** → made
8. **see** → saw
9. **take** → took
10. **come** → came

## Erros Comuns

### 1. Usar -ed em verbos irregulares

- ❌ *I goed to the store.* / *She buyed a car.*
- ✅ *I **went** to the store.* / *She **bought** a car.*

### 2. Usar a forma passada com *did*

- ❌ *Did you **went** to the party?*
- ✅ *Did you **go** to the party?*
- ❌ *She didn't **saw** the movie.*
- ✅ *She didn't **see** the movie.*

### 3. Confundir *said* e *told*

- *say* → não precisa de objeto: *She **said** goodbye.*
- *tell* → precisa de objeto (a quem): *She **told me** goodbye.*

### 4. Pronunciar *read* (passado) como presente

- Presente: *I read books every day.* /riːd/ (rima com "need")
- Passado: *I read a book yesterday.* /rɛd/ (rima com "red")

## Exemplos

### Diálogo — Viagem

```
A: Where did you go on vacation?
B: I went to Portugal. I spent two weeks there.
A: That sounds amazing! What did you do?
B: I saw many beautiful places. I ate incredible food and
   I drank amazing wine. I also met some wonderful people.
A: Did you buy any souvenirs?
B: Yes, I bought some tiles and a bottle of port wine.
A: Did you take many photos?
B: Of course! I took hundreds of photos. I can show you later.
```

### Narrativa — Um dia difícil

```
Yesterday was a terrible day. I woke up late because I forgot to set my alarm.
I ran to the bus stop, but the bus had already left. I took a taxi to work,
which cost a lot. When I got to the office, I found out I had a meeting
that I didn't know about. My boss told me about it, but I forgot.
After work, I went to the supermarket and bought some food for dinner.
I made pasta, but I put too much salt. I went to bed early because
I felt exhausted.
```
