## Conceitos Fundamentais

### Atividades da Manhã (*Morning Activities*)

| Inglês | Tradução | Exemplo |
|--------|----------|---------|
| **wake up** | acordar | I wake up at 6:30 AM. |
| **get up** | levantar-se | I get up at 6:45 AM. |
| **take a shower** | tomar banho | I take a shower every morning. |
| **brush my teeth** | escovar os dentes | I brush my teeth twice a day. |
| **get dressed** | se vestir | I get dressed for work. |
| **comb/brush my hair** | pentear o cabelo | She brushes her hair. |
| **have breakfast** | tomar café da manhã | I have breakfast at 7 AM. |
| **make coffee** | fazer café | He makes coffee every morning. |
| **leave home** | sair de casa | I leave home at 7:30. |
| **go to work/school** | ir para o trabalho/escola | She goes to work by bus. |

> **Wake up** vs. **Get up**: *Wake up* = abrir os olhos. *Get up* = sair da cama.

| **have a meeting** | ter uma reunião | We have a meeting at 10. |
| **have lunch** | almoçar | I have lunch at noon. |
| **take a break** | fazer uma pausa | Let's take a break. |
| **finish work** | terminar o trabalho | I finish work at 6 PM. |

### Expressões de Tempo para Rotinas

| Expressão | Tradução | Exemplo |
|-----------|----------|---------|
| in the morning | de manhã | I exercise **in the morning**. |
| in the afternoon | à tarde | She studies **in the afternoon**. |
| in the evening | à noite (início) | We watch TV **in the evening**. |
| at night | à noite | I read **at night**. |
| every day | todo dia | I work **every day**. |
| every week | toda semana | I clean the house **every week**. |
| on weekdays | nos dias de semana | I wake up early **on weekdays**. |
| on weekends | nos fins de semana | I sleep in **on weekends**. |
| once a week | uma vez por semana | I go to the gym **once a week**. |
| twice a day | duas vezes por dia | I brush my teeth **twice a day**. |
| three times a week | três vezes por semana | He exercises **three times a week**. |

## Regras e Exceções

### *Have* vs. *Take* vs. *Do* vs. *Make*

| Verbo | Combinações comuns |
|-------|-------------------|
| **have** | breakfast, lunch, dinner, a shower (BrE), a meeting, a break |
| **take** | a shower (AmE), a bath, a nap, a walk, a break |
| **do** | the dishes, the laundry, homework, exercise, housework |
| **make** | breakfast/lunch/dinner (cozinhar), coffee, the bed |

> **Diferença regional**: *have a shower* (BrE) vs. *take a shower* (AmE) — ambos corretos.

> **Make** vs. **Do**: *Make* = criar algo. *Do* = realizar uma atividade.

### Telling the Time (Dizendo as horas)

| Hora | Inglês | Alternativa |
|------|--------|-------------|
| 7:00 | seven o'clock | seven |
| 7:15 | seven fifteen | (a) quarter past seven |
| 7:30 | seven thirty | half past seven |
| 7:45 | seven forty-five | (a) quarter to eight |
| 8:05 | eight oh five | five past eight |
| 8:50 | eight fifty | ten to nine |
| 12:00 | twelve o'clock / noon | midday |
| 00:00 | twelve o'clock / midnight | — |

## Erros Comuns

### 1. Traduzir "tomar banho" literalmente

- ❌ *I take a bath every morning.* (bath = banho de banheira)
- ✅ *I **take a shower** every morning.* (shower = chuveiro)

### 2. Confundir *go to bed* e *go to sleep*

- *Go to bed* = ir para a cama (ação física)
- *Go to sleep* / *Fall asleep* = adormecer (começar a dormir)

### 3. Usar *do* quando deveria usar *make* (e vice-versa)

- ❌ *I do dinner.* → ✅ *I **make** dinner.* (cozinhar)
- ❌ *I make the dishes.* → ✅ *I **do** the dishes.* (lavar louça)

### 4. Esquecer *the* antes de substantivos específicos

- ❌ *I do dishes.* → ✅ *I do **the** dishes.*
- ❌ *I clean house.* → ✅ *I clean **the** house.*

## Exemplos

### Minha Rotina Diária

```
I wake up at 6:30 in the morning. I take a shower and brush my teeth.
Then I get dressed and have breakfast. I usually have coffee and toast.

I leave home at 7:30 and go to work by bus. I arrive at work at 8:30.
I start work at 9 AM. I check my emails and have meetings.

I have lunch at noon. I usually eat at a restaurant near the office.
I finish work at 6 PM and go home by bus.

I arrive home at 7 PM. I cook dinner and eat with my family.
After dinner, I do the dishes and watch TV.
I study English for 30 minutes every night.
I go to bed at 11 PM.

On weekends, I sleep in and clean the house. I go to the gym
on Saturday morning and hang out with friends on Sunday.
```

### Diálogo — Perguntando sobre rotina

```
A: What time do you wake up?
B: I usually wake up at 7 AM. What about you?
A: I wake up at 6. I go to the gym before work.
B: Wow! How often do you go to the gym?
A: Three times a week. Monday, Wednesday, and Friday.
B: That's great! What time do you finish work?
A: At 5 PM. Then I go home and cook dinner.
B: Do you cook every day?
A: Not every day. Sometimes I order food.
```
