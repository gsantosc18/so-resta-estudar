# Passado Simples — Verbos Regulares (*Simple Past — Regular Verbs*)

## Objetivo

Ao final deste tópico, o estudante será capaz de formar e usar o passado simples com verbos regulares em inglês, aplicando corretamente o sufixo *-ed* e suas regras de ortografia e pronúncia.

## Pré-requisitos

- [Simple Present](../01-a1-beginner/04-simple-present.md)
- [Pronomes Pessoais](../01-a1-beginner/02-personal-pronouns.md)

## Conceitos Fundamentais

### Quando usar o Simple Past

O passado simples descreve **ações concluídas** no passado, com tempo definido:

| Uso | Exemplo | Tradução |
|-----|---------|----------|
| **Ação concluída** | I worked yesterday. | Eu trabalhei ontem. |
| **Sequência de eventos** | She arrived and opened the door. | Ela chegou e abriu a porta. |
| **Hábitos passados** | We played soccer every weekend. | Nós jogávamos futebol todo fim de semana. |
| **Estado passado** | He lived in London for 3 years. | Ele morou em Londres por 3 anos. |

### Formação dos Verbos Regulares

Para verbos regulares, adiciona-se **-ed** ao verbo base:

| Regra | Verbo | Passado | Exemplo |
|-------|-------|---------|---------|
| Maioria: + **-ed** | work | work**ed** | I worked yesterday. |
| Termina em -e: + **-d** | live | live**d** | She lived in Paris. |
| Consoante + y: y → **-ied** | study | stud**ied** | He studied all night. |
| Vogal + y: + **-ed** | play | play**ed** | They played soccer. |
| CVC* (1 sílaba): dobrar + **-ed** | stop | stop**ped** | The bus stopped. |
| CVC* (2+ sílabas, tônica final): dobrar | prefer | prefer**red** | She preferred tea. |
| CVC* (tônica NÃO final): + **-ed** | visit | visit**ed** | We visited Rome. |

> *CVC = Consoante-Vogal-Consoante

### Forma Afirmativa

Estrutura: **Sujeito + verbo-ed + complemento**

> A forma é **igual para todos os sujeitos** — sem variação de pessoa:

```
I worked.        He worked.       She worked.
You worked.      We worked.       They worked.
```

```
I watched a movie last night.
She studied English for two hours.
They lived in Brazil for five years.
We visited our grandparents last weekend.
```

### Forma Negativa

Estrutura: **Sujeito + did not (didn't) + verbo (base) + complemento**

> **Importante**: Com *didn't*, o verbo volta à forma base (sem -ed):

```
I didn't work yesterday.        (✅ forma base)
I didn't worked yesterday.      (❌ duplo passado)

She didn't study last night.
They didn't watch TV.
We didn't live in São Paulo.
```

### Forma Interrogativa

Estrutura: **Did + sujeito + verbo (base) + complemento?**

```
Did you work yesterday?         → Você trabalhou ontem?
Did she study English?          → Ela estudou inglês?
Did they visit Rome?            → Eles visitaram Roma?
```

### Respostas Curtas

| Pergunta | Sim | Não |
|----------|-----|-----|
| Did you...? | Yes, I did. | No, I didn't. |
| Did she...? | Yes, she did. | No, she didn't. |
| Did they...? | Yes, they did. | No, they didn't. |

### Palavras-chave do Simple Past

| Expressão | Tradução |
|-----------|----------|
| yesterday | ontem |
| last night / last week / last month / last year | ontem à noite / semana passada / etc. |
| ... ago | ... atrás (two days ago, three years ago) |
| in 2020 | em 2020 |
| when I was young | quando eu era jovem |
| this morning (se já passou) | esta manhã |

## Pronúncia e Fonética

### As 3 pronúncias do -ed

Esta é uma das maiores dificuldades do passado regular. O -ed tem **3 pronúncias**:

| Pronúncia | Quando | Exemplos |
|-----------|--------|----------|
| **/t/** | Após sons surdos: /p/, /k/, /f/, /s/, /ʃ/, /tʃ/ | work**ed**, stopp**ed**, watch**ed**, wash**ed** |
| **/d/** | Após sons sonoros e vogais | play**ed**, study**ed**, live**d**, call**ed**, open**ed** |
| **/ɪd/** | Após /t/ ou /d/ | want**ed**, need**ed**, start**ed**, decid**ed** |

> **Regra prática**: 
> - Se o verbo termina em som de **t** ou **d** → /ɪd/ (sílaba extra)
> - Se o verbo termina em som **surdo** → /t/
> - Se o verbo termina em som **sonoro** ou vogal → /d/

#### Exemplos por pronúncia

| /t/ | /d/ | /ɪd/ |
|-----|-----|------|
| asked | answered | wanted |
| cooked | called | needed |
| finished | cleaned | started |
| helped | enjoyed | visited |
| liked | lived | decided |
| missed | loved | invited |
| stopped | opened | painted |
| talked | played | waited |
| walked | rained | hated |
| watched | studied | created |

> **Erro comum de brasileiros**: pronunciar *-ed* sempre como sílaba extra. Diga "wórkt" (worked), não "wór-ked". A sílaba extra só existe quando o verbo termina em /t/ ou /d/.

## Regras e Exceções

### O verbo *to be* NÃO usa *did*

O verbo *to be* tem suas próprias formas de passado:

| Presente | Passado |
|----------|---------|
| I am | I **was** |
| You are | You **were** |
| He/She/It is | He/She/It **was** |
| We are | We **were** |
| They are | They **were** |

```
❌ Did you were tired?
✅ Were you tired?

❌ I didn't was at home.
✅ I wasn't at home.
```

→ Verbos irregulares serão aprofundados em: [Simple Past — Irregular Verbs](02-simple-past-irregular.md)

## Comparações

### Simple Past vs. Simple Present

| Simple Present | Simple Past |
|---------------|-------------|
| Rotinas atuais | Ações concluídas no passado |
| I work every day. | I worked yesterday. |
| She studies English. | She studied English last year. |
| Do you play soccer? | Did you play soccer last weekend? |

## Erros Comuns

### 1. Duplo passado com *did*

- ❌ *Did you **watched** the movie?*
- ✅ *Did you **watch** the movie?*

### 2. Pronunciar -ed como sílaba extra sempre

- ❌ "I walk-ED to school." (2 sílabas para walked)
- ✅ "I walkt to school." (/wɔːkt/ — 1 sílaba)

### 3. Esquecer de usar *did* na interrogativa/negativa

- ❌ *You worked yesterday?* (compreensível, mas informal)
- ✅ *Did you work yesterday?*

### 4. Usar *did* com *was/were*

- ❌ *Did you were happy?*
- ✅ *Were you happy?*

### 5. Confundir ortografia com -y

- ❌ *He studyed all night.*
- ✅ *He stud**ied** all night.* (consoante + y → ied)
- ✅ *He play**ed** soccer.* (vogal + y → ed)

## Exemplos

### Diálogo — Fim de semana passado

```
A: What did you do last weekend?
B: I visited my parents in Campinas. We cooked a big lunch together.
A: That sounds nice! Did you stay the whole weekend?
B: No, I didn't. I returned on Sunday afternoon.
A: What did you do on Sunday evening?
B: I watched a movie and studied a little. And you?
A: I cleaned the house and played video games. Nothing special!
```
