# Comparativos e Superlativos (*Comparatives and Superlatives*)

## Objetivo

Ao final deste tópico, o estudante será capaz de formar e usar comparativos e superlativos para comparar pessoas, lugares, objetos e situações em inglês.

## Pré-requisitos

- [Artigos](../01-a1-beginner/03-articles-a-an-the.md)
- [Simple Present](../01-a1-beginner/04-simple-present.md)

## Conceitos Fundamentais

### Os três graus do adjetivo

| Grau | Uso | Exemplo |
|------|-----|---------|
| **Normal** | Descrever | She is tall. |
| **Comparativo** | Comparar dois | She is **taller** than me. |
| **Superlativo** | Destacar entre todos | She is **the tallest** in the class. |

### Regras de Formação

#### Adjetivos curtos (1 sílaba)

| Regra | Adj. | Comparativo | Superlativo |
|-------|------|------------|-------------|
| + **-er** / **-est** | tall | tall**er** | the tall**est** |
| + **-er** / **-est** | old | old**er** | the old**est** |
| + **-er** / **-est** | cheap | cheap**er** | the cheap**est** |
| Termina em -e: + **-r** / **-st** | nice | nice**r** | the nice**st** |
| CVC: dobrar + **-er** / **-est** | big | big**ger** | the big**gest** |
| CVC: dobrar + **-er** / **-est** | hot | hot**ter** | the hot**test** |
| CVC: dobrar + **-er** / **-est** | thin | thin**ner** | the thin**nest** |

#### Adjetivos longos (2+ sílabas)

| Regra | Adj. | Comparativo | Superlativo |
|-------|------|------------|-------------|
| **more** / **most** | expensive | **more** expensive | **the most** expensive |
| **more** / **most** | beautiful | **more** beautiful | **the most** beautiful |
| **more** / **most** | interesting | **more** interesting | **the most** interesting |
| **more** / **most** | comfortable | **more** comfortable | **the most** comfortable |

#### Adjetivos de 2 sílabas terminados em -y

| Regra | Adj. | Comparativo | Superlativo |
|-------|------|------------|-------------|
| -y → **-ier** / **-iest** | happy | happ**ier** | the happ**iest** |
| -y → **-ier** / **-iest** | easy | eas**ier** | the eas**iest** |
| -y → **-ier** / **-iest** | busy | bus**ier** | the bus**iest** |
| -y → **-ier** / **-iest** | early | earl**ier** | the earl**iest** |
| -y → **-ier** / **-iest** | funny | funn**ier** | the funn**iest** |

#### Adjetivos irregulares

| Adjetivo | Comparativo | Superlativo |
|----------|------------|-------------|
| **good** | **better** | **the best** |
| **bad** | **worse** | **the worst** |
| **far** | **farther/further** | **the farthest/furthest** |
| **old** | **older/elder** | **the oldest/eldest** |
| **much/many** | **more** | **the most** |
| **little** | **less** | **the least** |

### Usando Comparativos

Estrutura: **Adjetivo comparativo + than**

```
She is taller than her brother.
This book is more interesting than that one.
My car is faster than yours.
English is easier than Chinese.
```

> **Than** = do que (para comparação)

### Usando Superlativos

Estrutura: **the + adjetivo superlativo** (+ in/of)

```
She is the tallest in the class.
This is the most expensive restaurant in the city.
He is the best student of all.
It's the worst movie I've ever seen.
```

> Use **in** para lugares/grupos: *the tallest **in** the class*
> Use **of** para quantidades: *the best **of** all*

### Igualdade: *as...as*

Estrutura: **as + adjetivo (normal) + as**

```
She is as tall as her mother.        → Ela é tão alta quanto a mãe.
This book is as good as that one.    → Este livro é tão bom quanto aquele.
He isn't as fast as his brother.     → Ele não é tão rápido quanto o irmão.
```

> **Negativa** (*not as...as*) = "não tão...quanto" (indica que é menos):
> - *He isn't as tall as his brother.* = He is shorter.

### Intensificadores

| Intensificador | Uso | Exemplo |
|---------------|-----|---------|
| **much** / **a lot** | Diferença grande | She is **much** taller than me. |
| **a little** / **slightly** | Diferença pequena | He is **slightly** older than her. |
| **even** | Comparação enfática | This is **even** better! |
| **by far** | Superlativo enfático | She is **by far** the best. |

## Erros Comuns

### 1. Dupla comparação

- ❌ *She is **more taller** than me.*
- ✅ *She is **taller** than me.*
- ❌ *It's the **most cheapest** option.*
- ✅ *It's the **cheapest** option.*

### 2. Esquecer *the* no superlativo

- ❌ *She is tallest in the class.*
- ✅ *She is **the** tallest in the class.*

### 3. Esquecer *than* no comparativo

- ❌ *He is taller me.*
- ✅ *He is taller **than** me.*

### 4. Usar *more* com adjetivos curtos

- ❌ *She is **more** tall.*
- ✅ *She is tall**er**.*

### 5. Confundir *good-better-best* e *bad-worse-worst*

- ❌ *She is gooder than me.* / *The goodest.*
- ✅ *She is **better** than me.* / *The **best**.*

### 6. Escrever *funnier* como *funnyer*

- ❌ *funn**yer***
- ✅ *funn**ier*** (y → ier)

## Exemplos

### Diálogo — Comparando cidades

```
A: Which city do you prefer, São Paulo or Rio?
B: I think Rio is more beautiful than São Paulo.
A: I agree, but São Paulo is bigger and has more job opportunities.
B: That's true. São Paulo is also more expensive.
A: What about Curitiba? Some say it's the best city to live in Brazil.
B: I don't know. I've never been there. But I've heard it's 
   colder than both São Paulo and Rio!
A: Yes, it's the coldest capital in the south.
```

### Frases comparativas

```
My brother is older than me, but I am taller than him.
English is easier than Japanese, but not as easy as Spanish.
This is the best restaurant in town. It's much better than the others.
She is the most intelligent student in our class.
Today is hotter than yesterday. It's the hottest day of the year!
```

### Nível 2 — Complete com a forma correta

1. She is ______ (tall) than her sister.
2. This is the ______ (good) pizza I've ever had.
3. My house is ______ (big) than his apartment.
4. He is the ______ (intelligent) student in the class.
5. Today is ______ (hot) than yesterday.
6. This exercise is not as ______ (difficult) as the last one.

### Nível 3 — Traduza para o inglês

1. Ela é mais alta que eu.
2. Este é o melhor restaurante da cidade.
3. Meu irmão é tão inteligente quanto eu.
4. O inverno é a estação mais fria do ano.
5. Este livro é mais interessante que aquele.
6. Ela é a aluna mais aplicada da turma.
7. Este hotel é muito mais caro que o outro.
